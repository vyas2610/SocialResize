Official YouTube Thumbnail Specifications

According to YouTube's creator guidelines, high-performing thumbnails must satisfy specific technical parameters to display sharply on mobile phones, tablets, desktops, and TV screens:

Requirement Standard Specification Why It Matters
Resolution 1280 × 720 pixels (minimum width 640px) Guarantees 720p HD clarity across desktop and high-DPI displays.
Aspect Ratio 16:9 widescreen Standard player format; prevents black pillarbox bars on video embeds.
Maximum File Size Under 2 Megabytes (2MB) YouTube will strictly reject uploads larger than 2,097,152 bytes.
Recommended Format JPG (JPEG) High compression efficiency; comfortably stays below the 2MB threshold.

The Timecode Timestamp Safe Zone

In YouTube search results, home feeds, and recommended sidebars, YouTube overlays the video length timestamp in the bottom right corner of every thumbnail.

Crucial Tip: Never position small text, subtitles, key faces, or sponsor logos in the bottom right 20% of your thumbnail canvas, or they will be obstructed by the black timestamp box.

Why Convert PNG to JPG?

PNG files are lossless, which means every pixel is retained without compression artifacts. However, for continuous-tone photography, PNGs can be 5 to 10 times larger than necessary.

Key Benefits of Converting PNG to JPG
  • Dramatic File Size Reductions: A 4MB PNG photograph typically compresses down to approximately 400KB–600KB as an 85% quality JPG with almost no perceptible difference in visual clarity.
  • Faster Page & App Loading: Smaller file sizes load faster on mobile networks, reducing bounce rates and bandwidth consumption.
  • Controlled Transparency Fill: Because the JPEG specification does not support alpha transparency channels, SocialResize lets you choose the exact background matte color (default white) so transparent PNG cutouts render cleanly.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 1, 2026

Summary of Our Privacy Guarantee

SocialResize is engineered with a strict zero-server-upload architecture. Every photo you select is processed directly inside your browser using client-side JavaScript and HTML5 Canvas. Your images are never transmitted over the internet to our servers or third-party storage services.

1. Information We Do Not Collect (Image Privacy)

Unlike traditional online image converters that require uploading your photos to a remote cloud server, SocialResize operates entirely within your web browser's local sandbox:

  • No Image Uploads: When you drag or upload a photo, it is loaded into your computer's or mobile device's local memory via the standard browser FileReader API.
  • No Cloud Storage: We do not operate any backend servers, databases, or Amazon S3/Google Cloud buckets that receive, log, or store your images.
  • No EXIF/Metadata Harvesting: We do not extract, store, or sell personal location (GPS) data, camera models, or timestamps embedded in your photos.
  • Instant Deletion: When you close or refresh your browser tab, the temporary image objects in your device's RAM are immediately discarded by your operating system and browser garbage collector.

2. Information We Automatically Collect (Web Analytics)

To understand how visitors discover and navigate our website and to improve our tools, we may use privacy-conscious analytics services such as Google Analytics 4 (GA4):

  • General Site Metrics: We collect aggregated, non-personally identifiable information such as pages visited, browser type, device category (desktop, mobile, tablet), referring website, and time spent on the site.
  • Tool Feature Usage: We may track feature usage events (for example: "user clicked 1080x1920 Instagram Story preset" or "user downloaded JPG"). However, these events contain strictly generic numerical dimensions and file extensions—no filenames, photo contents, or image data are ever included in analytics payloads.
  • IP Anonymization: IP addresses are anonymized or masked by default before storage by Google Analytics.

3. Cookies and Local Storage

SocialResize minimizes the use of cookies and local storage tokens:

  • Theme Preferences: We use your browser's local storage (localStorage) strictly to save your preference for Light Mode or Dark Mode (theme: 'light' | 'dark'). This setting never leaves your browser.
  • Third-Party Cookies (Advertising): In the future, SocialResize may display contextual advertising through reputable ad networks such as Google AdSense to keep these tools 100% free for everyone. Advertising partners may use cookies or web beacons to serve personalized ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings or AboutAds.info.

4. Third-Party Links and Services

Our website contains informational guides and references to external social media platforms (such as Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, LinkedIn, TikTok, WhatsApp, X, and Pinterest). We have no control over the privacy policies, tracking mechanisms, or data collection practices of these external services. We encourage you to inspect their respective privacy statements when posting your resized media.

5. Children's Online Privacy Protection (COPPA)

SocialResize is a general audience utility tool intended for content creators, photographers, digital marketers, and social media users of all ages. Because we do not request or collect personal information, registration data, or uploaded media, we do not knowingly gather personally identifiable data from children under the age of 13.

6. Data Security

Because all image rendering and resizing occur entirely on your local machine, your creative assets are protected by your own device's security environment. No data in transit exists for your media files, eliminating risks associated with intercepting server communications or server data breaches.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect modifications in our tooling, browser security standards, or applicable privacy regulations. The "Last Updated" date at the top of this document will always reflect the most current revision.

8. Contacting Us

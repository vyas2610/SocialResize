Precision Pixel Control for Any Purpose

Whether you need an exact 800 × 600 px product mockup, a 300 × 250 px display advertisement banner, or a custom wallpaper for a dual-monitor setup, SocialResize’s custom dimension engine provides millimeter-precise pixel control.

Aspect Ratio Lock vs. Free Dimensions

  • Aspect Ratio Locked (Default): When enabled, editing either width or height automatically computes the complementary dimension to prevent stretching, squishing, or distortion.
  • Aspect Ratio Unlocked: Allows you to define entirely new bounds. Use Fill & Crop to adapt your image to the new frame cleanly, or Fit (Letterbox) to preserve the full photo inside the new bounds.

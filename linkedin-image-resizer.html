LinkedIn Sizing Standards for Creators & Brands

Unlike casual networks, LinkedIn posts and banners directly influence professional credibility. Using pixel-perfect dimensions prevents unsightly compression and awkward avatar overlaps:

Asset Type Standard Resolution Aspect Ratio Key Considerations
Feed Post 1200 × 627 px 1.91:1 Optimal for company updates and shared article links.
Personal Cover Banner 1584 × 396 px 4:1 Circular avatar covers the bottom-left area on desktop and mobile.
Company Page Cover 1128 × 191 px 5.91:1 Ultra-panoramic; keep text and tagline vertically centered.
Profile Picture 400 × 400 px 1:1 High-res headshot framed in a circular crop.

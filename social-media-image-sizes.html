Pro Sizing Rule for Mobile Screens

On feed platforms like Instagram and Facebook, portrait formats (4:5 or 1080×1350 px) occupy approximately 25% more screen real estate than square posts, which significantly boosts engagement and swipe-stop rate on mobile devices.

Platform-Specific Best Practices

1. Instagram Sizing Nuances

Instagram supports three primary feed aspect ratios: square (1:1), portrait (4:5), and landscape (1.91:1). All images uploaded to Instagram feeds are displayed within a profile grid cropped to a square (1:1). Keep your main visual focal point centered so the square grid preview looks natural.

  • Feed Portrait: 1080 × 1350 px (ideal for visibility)
  • Stories & Reels: 1080 × 1920 px (leave 250px at the top and bottom free from text to prevent interface overlay obstruction)

2. YouTube Thumbnail Guidelines

YouTube thumbnails require a 16:9 widescreen aspect ratio with standard 1280 × 720 px resolution. YouTube imposes a strict file size ceiling of 2MB for thumbnails. Keep titles large, high-contrast, and avoid placing important text in the bottom right corner where the video duration timestamp sits.

3. TikTok & Vertical Video Snapshots

TikTok uses full-screen vertical 9:16 media (1080 × 1920 px). While video is the primary format, photo mode slideshows have become massive engagement drivers. High-resolution JPG images resized to 1080 × 1920 display crisply on both iOS and Android.

Key Sizing Takeaways
  • Always size your images to the platform's exact native resolution before publishing.
  • Leave safe margins on vertical 9:16 formats (Stories, Reels, Shorts) to avoid icon collisions.
  • Export photographs as high-quality JPGs (~85% quality) to balance sharpness with fast loading.
  • Use PNG for graphic logos, text-heavy banners, and illustrations requiring transparency.

Key Facebook Dimensions Explained

Facebook features multiple presentation layouts across mobile and desktop. Resizing with specific dimensions ensures your content displays sharply across all screens:

Placement Recommended Dimensions Aspect Ratio Format Recommendation
Feed Post (Timeline) 1200 × 630 px 1.91:1 JPG (high clarity & quick feed loading)
Page Cover Photo 820 × 312 px 2.63:1 PNG (avoids JPEG compression around brand text)
Story 1080 × 1920 px 9:16 JPG
Profile Picture 170 × 170 px 1:1 PNG

The Desktop vs. Mobile Cover Photo Dilemma

Facebook displays Page Cover photos at 820 × 312 pixels on desktop computers, but crops the left and right sides to render at 640 × 360 pixels on smartphones.

To ensure your banner looks sharp on both, maintain an 820px wide canvas and keep all core contact details, slogans, and logos centered within the inner 640px wide safe zone.

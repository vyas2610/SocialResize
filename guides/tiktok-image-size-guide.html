TikTok Photo Mode & Carousel Dimensions (2026): 1080x1920 Guide

Published by SocialResize • Updated for 2026 • 5 min read

TikTok Photo Essentials

  • Photo Mode Resolution: 1080 x 1920 px (Full vertical 9:16)
  • Bottom Safe Margin: Leave 350 px clear for username, audio tag, and caption text.
  • Right Safe Margin: Leave 120 px clear for like, comment, and bookmark buttons.
  • File Format: JPG / PNG

Why TikTok Photo Mode Is Booming

TikTok Photo Mode allows users to swipe through slideshows of still photos set to trending audio tracks. These posts regularly generate high watch times and shares because viewers control the reading speed.

However, posting horizontal photos (16:9) causes TikTok to place black letterboxing bars above and below your image, drastically reducing immersion. By scaling every slide to 1080 x 1920 pixels, your photos fill the entire device screen seamlessly.

Interface Safe Zones on TikTok

Because the TikTok user interface is rich with overlays, keep your visual focal points centered:

  • Top 150 px: Search icon and "Following / For You" tabs.
  • Bottom 350 px: Your account name, post description, and audio title.
  • Right 120 px: Engagement buttons (Like heart, Comment bubble, Save bookmark, Share arrow).

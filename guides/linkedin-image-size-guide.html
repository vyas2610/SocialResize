LinkedIn Image Dimensions (2026): Posts, Banners & Pages

Published by SocialResize • Updated for 2026 • 5 min read

LinkedIn Standards at a Glance

  • Feed Post (Landscape): 1200 x 627 px (1.91:1)
  • Feed Post (Square): 1080 x 1080 px (1:1)
  • Personal Profile Banner: 1584 x 396 px (4:1)
  • Company Page Banner: 1128 x 191 px
  • Profile Photo: 400 x 400 px

Profile Banner Safe Zones: Avoid the Profile Photo Cutoff

Your personal LinkedIn background banner (1584 x 396 pixels) is one of the highest-visibility branding assets on the web. However, many professionals accidentally put important accreditation logos or tagline text right where their round headshot sits.

  • Desktop Overlay: On desktop screens, your circular profile picture covers the bottom-left quadrant of your banner (approximately 250px wide by 120px tall).
  • Mobile Overlay: In the LinkedIn mobile app, the profile photo shifts closer toward the center-left.
  • Best Practice: Position all key value propositions, book covers, website URLs, and company logos in the right 50% of the canvas.

Why Square & Portrait Posts Are Outperforming Landscape on LinkedIn

While 1200x627 remains the standard link preview dimension, native image posts uploaded as 1080 x 1080 (1:1) or 1080 x 1350 (4:5) occupy significantly more vertical space in the LinkedIn mobile mobile feed.

Because more than 70% of LinkedIn consumption now occurs on mobile devices, portrait posts provide superior visual clarity for infographics, quotes, and slides.

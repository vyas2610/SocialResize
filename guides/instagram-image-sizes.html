Instagram Image Sizes (2026): The Complete Creator Guide

Published by SocialResize • Updated for 2026 • 6 min read

Key Takeaways for 2026

  • Best Feed Format: 1080 x 1350 px (4:5 Portrait) delivers up to 30% more screen presence than traditional square posts.
  • Max Upload Width: Instagram scales any image wider than 1080px down, which often triggers aggressive compression blur.
  • Stories & Reels: Always use 1080 x 1920 px (9:16) with a 250px safe zone at top and bottom.
  • Color Space: Export strictly in sRGB to prevent desaturated or washed-out tones on mobile screens.

Why 4:5 Portrait Is the King of the Feed

In mobile social feeds, visual real estate directly correlates with dwell time and engagement. When a user scrolls on a smartphone (most commonly on displays with 19.5:9 or 20:9 screen ratios), a horizontal (16:9) photo takes up less than 35% of the viewport. In contrast, a 4:5 portrait photo (1080 x 1350 px) occupies nearly the entire vertical viewport before the caption begins.

This maximizes impact, prevents other posts from distracting the viewer, and gives your photography dramatic presence.

How to Avoid Instagram's Aggressive Compression

Many creators wonder why high-resolution 48-megapixel photos shot on professional cameras end up blurry after uploading. The cause is Instagram's automated resizing pipeline:

  1. Resolution Mismatch: If you upload a 6000 x 4000 px photo, Instagram's server must downscale it by more than 75% in real time, throwing away micro-contrast.
  2. Pre-Resizing to 1080px Width: By using our client-side Instagram Resizer, you control the bicubic scaling algorithm right in your browser. When Instagram receives an image that is already exactly 1080px wide, it applies minimal compression.
  3. High Quality Upload Setting: In the Instagram mobile app, navigate to Settings → Account → Data Usage and toggle "Upload at highest quality" on.

Instagram Carousel Sizing Rules

Instagram carousels (slideshow posts) can contain up to 10 photos or videos. A critical technical limitation: All slides in a carousel inherit the aspect ratio of the very first slide.

If your first slide is a 1:1 square, every subsequent 4:5 portrait slide will be automatically cropped into a square. To ensure consistency across all slides, crop and resize every photo in your carousel to identical 1080x1350 or 1080x1080 dimensions before uploading.

Summary Checklist for Perfect Instagram Posts

  • Width: Exactly 1080 pixels
  • Height: 1350 pixels (for portrait feed) or 1920 pixels (for Story/Reel)
  • Format: JPG with 85–90% compression quality
  • Color Profile: sRGB

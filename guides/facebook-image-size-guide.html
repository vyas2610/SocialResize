Facebook Image Sizes (2026): Feed, Covers, Events & Stories

Published by SocialResize • Updated for 2026 • 6 min read

Facebook Dimensions Quick Summary

  • Feed Shared Post: 1200 x 630 px (1.91:1) or 1080 x 1350 px (4:5 portrait)
  • Page Cover Photo: 820 x 312 px (desktop) / 640 x 360 px (mobile safe)
  • Event Cover: 1200 x 628 px (2:1)
  • Facebook Story: 1080 x 1920 px (9:16)
  • Group Cover: 1640 x 856 px (1.91:1)

Solving the Desktop vs Mobile Cover Photo Dilemma

Facebook page cover photos present a major layout puzzle for designers: on a computer browser, the cover renders wide and short at 820 x 312 pixels. However, inside the Facebook smartphone app, the cover renders taller and narrower at 640 x 360 pixels.

To ensure your cover photo looks sharp and complete on both platforms:

  1. Create an image canvas sized at 820 x 360 pixels.
  2. Place your logo, text headline, and contact details inside the center 640 x 312 pixel safe zone.
  3. Keep the left and right 90px strips free of important details (they will be visible on desktop, but cropped off on mobile).

How to Stop Facebook From Destroying Image Quality

Facebook applies some of the most aggressive compression in social media. Follow these technical guidelines to preserve image clarity:

  • Use PNG for Logos & Graphics: If your image contains typography, sharp lines, or solid branding colors, export as a PNG. Facebook compresses PNGs significantly less aggressively than JPEGs.
  • Pre-Size to Exactly 1200px or 2048px: Facebook natively supports three widths: 720px, 960px, and 2048px. For feed photos, 1200px or 2048px triggers the cleanest interpolation.
  • Always Use sRGB: Images exported with AdobeRGB or ProPhoto color spaces will turn dull and greenish once uploaded to Facebook.

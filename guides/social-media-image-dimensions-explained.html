Social Media Aspect Ratios & Pixels Explained (2026 Guide)

Published by SocialResize • Updated for 2026 • 7 min read

What Is an Aspect Ratio?

An aspect ratio represents the proportional relationship between an image's width and height (Width : Height). It is independent of actual pixel resolution. For instance, a 1080x1080 image and a 2048x2048 image have different pixel counts, but they share the identical 1:1 square aspect ratio.

The Master Aspect Ratio Cheat Sheet

Aspect Ratio Standard Resolution Primary Use Cases
1:1 (Square) 1080 x 1080 px Instagram Grid, Profile Avatars, Facebook Square Posts
4:5 (Vertical) 1080 x 1350 px Instagram Feed Posts, LinkedIn Portrait Posts
9:16 (Full Vertical) 1080 x 1920 px Stories (IG/FB), Reels, TikTok, Shorts, WhatsApp Status
16:9 (Landscape) 1280 x 720 / 1920 x 1080 px YouTube Thumbnails, X Posts, Standard Video
2:3 (Vertical) 1000 x 1500 px Pinterest Standard Pins, 35mm Photography
1.91:1 (Horizontal) 1200 x 628 / 1200 x 630 px Facebook Link Shares, LinkedIn Link Shares, Twitter Cards

Pixels vs Display Scaling

A pixel (short for "picture element") is the smallest physical dot of color on a display. Modern smartphones use high-density Retina or AMOLED screens with 300 to 500+ Pixels Per Inch (PPI). On these screens, a device pixel ratio of 2x or 3x scales images up smoothly.

By supplying source images that match high-definition dimensions (like 1080px or 1280px), you ensure that high-PPI smartphone displays render every fine detail sharply.

Instagram Reel Cover Size (2026): 9:16 & 1:1 Profile Safe Zones

Published by SocialResize • Updated for 2026 • 5 min read

Reel Cover Essentials

  • Full Canvas Resolution: 1080 x 1920 px (9:16)
  • Main Profile Grid Crop: Exactly the center 1080 x 1080 px (1:1)
  • Vertical Safe Margins: 420 px top margin and 420 px bottom margin.
  • Recommended Format: JPG under 4 MB.

The Two-in-One Challenge of Reel Covers

Creating an effective Instagram Reel cover image is trickier than standard posts because your graphic lives in two distinct locations with differing proportions:

  1. The Reels Tab & Feed (9:16 vertical): When users browse the dedicated Reels feed or view the post in full screen, your cover appears as a complete 1080 x 1920 vertical canvas.
  2. Your Main Profile Grid (1:1 square): When a visitor lands on your profile, Instagram crops your Reel cover to a center 1:1 square (1080 x 1080 px).

If your title text or focal face is positioned too high or too low, it gets ruthlessly chopped off on your profile grid, leaving an amateur-looking feed.

The Exact Safe Zone Math

To ensure your Reel thumbnail looks cohesive everywhere, apply this simple formula:

  • Total Image Height: 1920 pixels
  • Profile Square Height: 1080 pixels (centered vertically)
  • Top Invisible Buffer: (1920 - 1080) ÷ 2 = 420 pixels
  • Bottom Invisible Buffer: 420 pixels

By placing all headline text, product logos, and subject faces within the y = 420 px to y = 1500 px window, your thumbnail will display cleanly in both full 9:16 view and 1:1 profile tiles.

Right-Edge UI Elements (The Engagement Overlay)

When your Reel is viewed in the dedicated Reels tab, Instagram layers white action icons along the right edge of the screen: the Like heart, Comment bubble, Share paper airplane, and spinning audio disc.

Keep important visual focal points at least 120 pixels away from the right-hand border of the canvas to prevent these interaction buttons from obscuring your design.
